Spring break 2027 · Working plan

Desert week

April 17–25 · Las Vegas, Nevada

Eight nights in three acts: family climbing at Red Rock, two easy resort nights on the Strip, then Hoover Dam and a one-way paddle through Black Canyon.

The shape of the trip

Three stays, each with a job

  1. 3 nights Near Red Rock Sat 17 → Tue 20 Climb early. Recover slowly.
  2. 2 nights Mandalay Bay Tue 20 → Thu 22 Pool, Strip and a show.
  3. 3 nights Henderson Thu 22 → Sun 25 Dam, desert and river.

Day by day

The proposed itinerary

Stay 01

Red Rock base

Saturday night through Monday night · 3 nights

Sat 17 April

Arrive and settle in

Fly to Las Vegas, collect the rental car and check into a two- or three-bedroom rental within about 25 minutes of the Scenic Drive gate. Groceries, gear and an early night.

Sleep: Rental near Red Rock · night 1 of 3

Sun 18 April

First big morning

Calico climbing at sunrise

East Coast time works in our favor. Reach the gate around 5:30, enter at 6:00 and walk to Magic Bus or another familiar Calico crag. A handful of family routes is enough. Leave before the day gets crowded; pool and nap afterward.

Plan: First wave at the gate · no timed-entry reservation needed before 8:00

Red Rock visitor information ↗
Mon 19 April

Second Red Rock morning

Another early Calico session. If Sunday is unusually smooth, this could become the canyon day; otherwise keep the objective simple and let the kids build momentum. The afternoon stays open for swimming and recovery.

Sleep: Rental near Red Rock · night 3 of 3

Tue 20 April

One more adventure, then the Strip

Pack the car and choose between a short third climbing morning or the two-hour Canyon Rim horseback ride. Continue to Mandalay Bay for check-in, lazy river time and dinner.

Canyon Rim ride ↗ Mandalay Bay ↗

Sleep: Mandalay Bay · night 1 of 2

Stay 02

Mandalay Bay

Tuesday night through Wednesday night · 2 nights

Wed 21 April

Rest day, properly done

Beach, lazy river and a Vegas show

No alarm and no driving objective. Spend the day at Mandalay Bay Beach, use Shark Reef if the weather turns, then see KÀ or Blue Man Group in the evening.

Mandalay Bay Beach ↗ KÀ ↗ Blue Man Group ↗

Sleep: Mandalay Bay · night 2 of 2

Thu 22 April

Pool morning, heavy machinery afternoon

Have a slow breakfast and one last swim, then check out and head south to Dig This. The kids can operate a six-ton excavator or skid steer before the drive to Henderson.

Explore Dig This ↗

Sleep: Henderson rental · night 1 of 3

Stay 03

Henderson base

Thursday night through Saturday night · 3 nights

Fri 23 April

Bighorn sheep, Hoover Dam and the old railroad

Start at Hemenway Park, tour Hoover Dam, eat lunch in Boulder City and walk through several Historic Railroad Trail tunnels. There is no need to force the full 7.4-mile round trip.

Hoover Dam tours ↗ Railroad tunnels ↗

Note: The full Guided Dam Tour is currently first-come, first-served on site.

Sat 24 April

The river day

Black Canyon with Blazin’ Paddles

Take the first Half-Day Deluxe departure: UTV transfer, a short slot-canyon hike and a guided one-way paddle through Black Canyon, with Emerald Cave, a beach stop and lunch along the way. Return to the rental for the pool and an easy final dinner.

Blazin’ Paddles Half-Day Deluxe ↗

Book: First available departure · ages 8+

Sun 25 April

Breakfast, pack and fly home

Keep departure day simple. Check out, return the rental car and head home before school resumes Monday.

What the rentals need

Space, legal listings and a warm pool

Near Red Rock

Two or three bedrooms, within roughly 25 minutes of the Scenic Drive gate. It does not need to be in Summerlin proper. Verify short-term-rental registration and pool-heating terms.

Henderson

Two or three bedrooms in southeastern Henderson—Mission Hills, River Mountain or Black Mountain—with a genuinely heated pool or hot tub and current city registration.
