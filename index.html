Fri Apr 16 → Tue Apr 20 · 4 nights

Near Red Rock

Family climbing, hikes and room to spread out.

Fri 16
Land at 9:05 pm. Collect the rental car and check in. Arrange late arrival access; leave groceries for Saturday.
Sat 17
Easy first day. A short Red Rock hike, food shopping and gear sorting. Keep it flexible after the late flight; save the main climbing sessions for Sunday and Monday.
Sun 18
Early Calico session. Use East Coast body clocks to get an early start. Magic Bus and a few routes would be a successful morning. A quiet afternoon afterward.
Mon 19
Another climbing morning. Calico again, or a canyon/trad objective if everyone is keen. Leave the afternoon open for an easy hike, downtime or a Vegas show.
Tue 20
One more outing before moving. A short climb or Cowboy Trail Rides, then drive to Mandalay Bay.
Base: A two- or three-bedroom rental within roughly 25 minutes of the Scenic Drive gate. Prioritize location and space; a heated pool or hot tub is a bonus. The Summerlin condo under consideration advertises a clubhouse pool and hot tub; pool heating and April guest access remain unconfirmed.
Climbing timing & rental notes

Plan around the Scenic Drive's currently published 6 am opening. Recheck the April 2027 opening and timed-entry rules before the trip. Early arrival improves the odds but does not guarantee a parking spot or quiet routes; no need to turn a family morning into a queue-time contest.

Confirm any rental's pool temperature, access hours and heating charge before counting on swimming. For the condo, ask about clubhouse access for short stays as well.

Base: Two nights for the full pool experience. Check the room setup for four and the 2027 pool calendar before booking.

Thu Apr 22 → Sat Apr 24 · 2 nights

Henderson & Black Canyon

Machines, the dam and a day on the river.

Thu 22
Dig This, then settle in. Choose an age-appropriate excavator or skid-steer experience for the kids, then check into the rental.
Fri 23
Black Canyon kayaking with Blazin’ Paddles. The Half-Day Deluxe currently lists 7:00 and 10:30 am departures, with about four hours on the water plus transfers. Paddle, return to the rental for a shower, and keep the evening easy. Confirm the 2027 schedule and pickup plan before booking.
Sat 24
Hoover Dam and Boulder City, then the 10:55 pm flight. Check out with the car packed, visit the dam in the morning, then lunch, Hemenway Park's bighorn sheep or the railroad tunnels as energy allows. Leave time for dinner, fuel and the rental-car return; aim to reach the terminal around 8:55 pm.
Sun 25
Arrive Boston at 8:55 am. Change planes in Raleigh–Durham; the connection is 38 minutes. Keep the rest of Sunday free to recover.
Why Friday for the river: It leaves time to shower and dry gear at the rental, and avoids tying a water tour to the departure day. Saturday's dam visit can be shortened if everyone is tired or the day runs long.
River tour & lodging notes

The Half-Day Deluxe currently combines a UTV transfer, a 15–20 minute slot-canyon hike and a guided four-mile paddle, with Emerald Cave and lunch. The operator lists a minimum age of 8, so both children meet the current minimum. Confirm the April 2027 route, departures, meeting point and return time directly with the outfitter. The Hoover Dam Visitor Center and tours currently open daily at 9:00 am; the guided Dam Tour is sold on-site, first come first served.

Look for a two- or three-bedroom rental in southeastern Henderson or around Boulder City. A hot tub or confirmed heated pool would be useful; compare the full cost with heating included.
